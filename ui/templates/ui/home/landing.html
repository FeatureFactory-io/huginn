Welcome to Huginn

Human-AI Command Composite for Engineering Project Managers

Huginn ingests development signals from GitLab and Jira hourly, computes Master Variables — TRANSPARENCY, THROUGHPUT, CYCLE TIME, REWORK, QUALITY, COMPLEXITY, CONTRIBUTION — and produces a daily SitRep at morning planning so you know exactly where to spend your attention.

Situational Awareness
