How it works

Encode your situation assessment heuristics

Author Rules of Engagement — a versioned Playbook with Workflow and Variables that define what good looks like for each project. Gjallarhorn evaluates every sync against those expectations.

Feed deep, broad data streams

Connect GitLab, Jira, and more. Mimir playbooks extend ingestion with unprecedented breadth and depth — commits, issues, milestones, and the signals your Variables need to assess reality.

See priorities immediately

Color-coded Variables on the Tactical Plot surface red, orange, yellow, and green instantly. Track progression over time via VariableDatapoints — each point traces to the SitRep that produced it.

SitReps that drive decisions

Gjallarhorn sits on top of your data streams — situational assessment, Variables snapshot, and Decisions with Outcome options. You choose the course: calibrate doctrine, dispatch work, or type your own.

Learns from your judgment

Every Decision review enriches Decisions Logic — project memory of how reasoning evolved — and your Commander profile. Gjallarhorn adapts proposed Outcomes to your management style over time.

Unleash autonomous mode

When RoE and management style are sufficiently aligned, flip Semi-Auto to Auto per project. Gjallarhorn executes Outcomes on its own — you observe via audit log and Action Stations.
