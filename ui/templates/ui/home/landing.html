The daily command loop · Acts 5–12

From signal to accountable action.

Gjallarhorn assembles the situation. You bring judgment. Huginn preserves the evidence, executes the chosen outcome, and carries the learning into the next loop.

05 · Orient

Read the situation, not another dashboard.

Each sync becomes a frozen SitRep: assessed period, Rules of Engagement, Variables, applied FRAGOs, and the Decisions that now deserve command attention.

SitRep · Evidence · Proposed Decisions
06 · Calibrate

Change the doctrine without erasing the record.

A FRAGO explains a legitimate exception in flight. Scope it, time-box it, and verify exactly which SitReps used it.

Project-scoped · Versioned · Auditable
07 · Explain

See when the signal changed—and why.

Every Variable trajectory is reconstructed from immutable SitRep datapoints. Select any point to inspect its period, source plan, and original assessment.

Variable trajectory · Provenance · Drill-down
08 · Interrogate

Ask the question the report did not answer.

Gjallarhorn reasons inside the selected Project, grounds its answer in live evidence, and keeps tool work inspectable instead of hiding it behind a confident paragraph.

Grounded in SitRep · Project memory · Tool trace
09 · Decide

Choose the outcome—and leave the reasoning behind.

Approve one bounded calibration or dispatch, reject with optional vigilance, or write your own. The action and your rationale become durable command memory.

Outcome options · Human judgment · Decisions Logic
10 · Verify reality

Inspect contribution without collapsing people into a score.

Follow the underlying increments and work items day by day. Identity reconciliation keeps source handles together while the evidence remains available for review.

Activity timeline · Reconciled identity · Source records
11 · Confirm dispatch

Know that the work landed where execution happens.

Action Stations is a read-only Jira mirror for successful dispatches. Huginn shows current state and provenance; the team keeps working in its system of record.

Read-only Jira mirror · Dispatch provenance · Sync
12 · Compound

Carry today’s context into every future loop.

Situational Awareness is workspace-wide narrative memory: durable constraints, active situations, source Decisions, and a version trail that explains what changed.

Workspace-wide narrative memory · Versions · Decision links
